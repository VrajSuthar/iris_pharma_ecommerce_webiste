Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Iris Pharma ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website and place an order, and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we ask for:

  • Your full name
  • Your phone number (used to send order details via WhatsApp)
  • Your delivery address

We also store your cart contents locally in your browser (localStorage) so items stay in your cart between visits. This data never leaves your device until you check out.

How we use your information

  • To confirm and fulfil your order over WhatsApp
  • To arrange delivery to the address you provide
  • To respond to questions you send us on WhatsApp
  • To process online payments (see below)

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Online payments

If you choose to pay online, your payment is processed by Razorpay, a licensed payment gateway. Iris Pharma never sees or stores your full card, UPI or banking details — those are handled directly by Razorpay on their secure, PCI-DSS compliant systems. We only receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, along with a payment reference ID.

You can also choose the WhatsApp checkout flow instead, with payment (UPI or cash before dispatch) arranged directly with you over chat.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Message us on WhatsApp or write to us at the address in our footer.
