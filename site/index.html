Good to know

How do I pay for my order?

You can pay securely online at checkout (cards, UPI or netbanking, via Razorpay), or choose the WhatsApp flow — your order details open in WhatsApp and we confirm the total and payment (UPI or cash before dispatch) right there in chat.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Our formulas are free from parabens, sulphates and silicones. If you have a specific skin condition, message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll help you choose.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders within Maharashtra arrive in 2–4 working days; the rest of India typically takes 4–7 working days. Exact timelines are shared when your order is confirmed.

Can I return a product?

Yes — see our Refund & Cancellation Policy for details on eligibility and how to start a return over WhatsApp.
