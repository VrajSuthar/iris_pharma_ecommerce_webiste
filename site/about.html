Our story

Ayurveda for the everyday, made with care

Iris Pharma started with a simple frustration: skincare shelves full of promises, short on honesty about what's actually inside the bottle.

We went back to what Indian households have trusted for generations — turmeric, neem, aloe vera — and paired those ingredients with careful, small-batch formulation. No harsh chemicals, no confusing ingredient lists. Just herbal formulas that work, secure online checkout, and a real person a WhatsApp chat away if you have a question before you buy.
